# CRM360 Research Notes

Research was performed on August 2, 2026 against the public pages linked below. The goal was to extract workflow patterns, not copy visuals or proprietary assets.

## CRM patterns

| Product | Useful pattern | Visible complexity / weakness | CRM360 decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HubSpot | Contacts, deals, tasks, reminders, and reporting are presented as a connected CRM workflow; the task dashboard acts as a personalized command center. | The platform spans marketing, sales, and service, which can create more configuration than a small team needs. | Adopt connected records and task visibility; keep CRM360 focused on sales relationships and follow-through. |
| Pipedrive | The pipeline is activity-centered: leads can be qualified into pipeline work, stages are visually grouped, and overdue activities receive attention cues. | Multiple pipelines, add-ons, automations, and reporting can become a larger operating model. | Adopt explicit stages, owners, next actions, and overdue cues; keep one reliable pipeline for CRM360. |
| Zoho CRM | Broad CRM suites commonly expose strong customization and module coverage. | More modules and configuration increase onboarding burden for smaller teams. | Keep the navigation limited to the daily relationship loop. |
| Attio | Sidebar orientation, global search, records, tasks, notifications, quick actions, and relationship context are first-class workspace concepts. | Flexible blank-slate data models require teams to decide their own structure before getting value. | Adopt a clear sidebar/search structure; provide a predefined CRM360 model and Focus Queue. |
| Linear | Calm hierarchy, reduced visual noise, keyboard-friendly navigation, clear statuses, and action-oriented issue views. | Product/work tracking patterns do not directly cover customer relationship data. | Adopt restraint, density, and status clarity without copying a product UI. |

## User activation and onboarding

The A/B Test page emphasizes concrete experiments around onboarding checklists, clear CTA wording, progressive disclosure, and transparent value communication. CRM360 therefore leads with the activation loop—create a lead, assign an owner, set a next action, and see it in the Focus Queue—instead of adding a long onboarding tour or invented pricing.

## Motion and 3D references

The named portfolio and scroll references demonstrate that React/Three.js, Spline, Framer Motion, GSAP, and scroll-driven composition can create strong visual moments. They also have materially different performance and implementation costs from a CRM productivity app. CRM360 uses one text-free material image, layered record-card forms, short transitions, and a small connection-plane scene on the public landing page. The authenticated workspace uses CSS depth and subtle motion rather than a heavy WebGL scene, with reduced-motion-friendly behavior.

## UI kit references

MeetUI, Aceternity/Shadcn examples, Portfolios.world, Pixa AI, and HorizonX were treated as pattern references for component consistency, bento-like grouping, crisp cards, CTA hierarchy, and motion restraint. No paid assets or copied logos are included. CRM360 uses original components and tokens.
